# Bitter Lesson sort, slice 2a: parts 1 to 16

**Provenance.** Built from the banked census and longitudinal findings, the registry, KILLED_MECHANISMS, the register and the knowledge archive, plus one structural fact from another estate (that it carries a generated-index tool), verified first-hand by file listing on 2026-10-06. Banked on the User's word, 2026-10-06, which clears that fact (CD #4e).

**What this is.** For each unit agreed at slice 1, the kinds it could belong to, held neutral. Each option states its kind, its assumption with the assumption's type, and the direction it expects. Each unit then names one test that tells its options apart, and says which outcome refutes which option. The User assigns each unit at slice 3, or defers it. Parts 17 to 32 follow in slice 2b.

## Conventions

- **Kinds (slice 1).** *Prescribed procedure*: the part fixes the steps; expected to fade, meaning the written part stops being needed. *Judgement practice*: the part names an outcome and leaves the form to the model; expected to hold or grow, meaning the written part keeps shaping the work. *Independent of the model*: the part answers to the record, the User's authority, confidentiality or the language standard; expected to hold.
- **Assumption types and clocks (slice 1).** A *capability* assumption is reviewed at a change of the relevant model's generation, dated by telemetry, and that review re-runs the census. The relevant model is the main thread's, except where a part turns on delegated agents (7, 8): there the delegate tier's generation is the clock (the User, 2026-10-06). A *setting* assumption is reviewed at a harness change that touches it. The release-cadence check flags each new harness version; whether a release touches a setting assumption is judged from its release notes and put to the User. An option *independent of the model* triggers no review at a model change; it is checked at the scheduled re-run on 2027-01-03.
- **Falsifiers.** A falsifier is an observed outcome, with its instrument and its time, that contradicts the option's expected direction. A unit's test discriminates when its options expect different outcomes of it.
- **Threshold** (kept by the User, 2026-10-06). Where a test compares a rate with the baseline: a change of a half or more against the 2026-10-03 census reading, per active session, in an estate with at least ten sessions on each side of the comparison. Smaller changes leave every option standing.
- **Authority options** have no behavioural falsifier. They are refuted only by a dated decision of the User, visible in git as a change to the governing text. That is stated in each such unit.
- **Evidence and its limits.** Sources: the census (`finding-exercise-census-2026-10-03.md`, *census*); the longitudinal finding (`finding-longitudinal-evidence-2026-10-03.md`, receipt codes R1 to R19); the registry; KILLED_MECHANISMS; the register (TOOLING_TRIGGERS); the knowledge archive. Every limit of those findings carries over. Being exercised shows a part is done, which under governance includes compliance, so use is weak evidence of need; trials test need directly. The one measured model switch rests on about a dozen sessions a side and coincides with time passing. Rows marked *scout reading* are claims until read first-hand. Hook silence is a missing reading.
- **Test types.** *Re-run*: the census on 2027-01-03 and at each relevant generation change. *Trial*: a bounded task with the part absent and present, outcomes named by the User in advance, run after the slice 3 ruling as its own undertaking. *Shadow*: a control left running in log-only mode. An instrument marked *owed* does not exist yet.
- **Ladder** (optional tag from the controls model): block, ask, advise, watch, written guidance.
