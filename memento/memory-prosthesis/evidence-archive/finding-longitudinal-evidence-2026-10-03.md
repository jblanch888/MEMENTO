# Finding: Memento's parts across model generations, 2025 to 2026

**Provenance.** Sources: local records on the User's machine (session transcripts and prompt history, Claude Code telemetry and its collector log, governance-hook logs, git histories and their AI co-author trailers, an estate's model-upgrade trials, and the 2025 notes and reports from Memento's first months), read on 2026-10-03. Counting by scripts that emit counts, identifiers and dates; reading of the 2025 notes and reports and of the trial records by smart-tier scouts returning fixed-field summaries with no quotation. Fitting: private folder names, sub-project names, session labels and identifying material removed or excluded; activity reported as shapes and rates. Each claim carries a receipt code; the receipts index (paths, scripts, queries and figures) is held outside the repository at `~/.memento/census/receipts-2026-10-03.md`, with the scripts and the full scout reports beside it. De-identified; cleared by the User, 2026-10-03 ("yes", on draft r2 after an independent review whose findings were all applied).

**Origin.** The User, 2026-10-03, after the exercise census: telemetry records model versions, his use moved across models at known dates, and older evidence exists on the machine. He asked for a wide recon-tier search, then for everything to be banked with links to its receipts and shared with build-protocols/dev.

## Method

- **Discovery:** seventeen recon-tier scouts inventoried the machine for older evidence, reporting stores, formats and date ranges with no content (R17). Scout figures are leads; the load-bearing ones were re-grounded first-hand, as marked in the receipts index.
- **Counting, content-free:** prompt history joined to estates by session (R3); telemetry model ids, main-thread models and first-seen dates (R5, R6); the telemetry collector log from June 2026 for gates that blocked, hook runs and named agents (R8); governance-hook outcomes by month (R9); per-session transcript rates across a model switch (R7); git file effects and the working context's active-playbook line (R4); AI co-author trailers across all repositories (R11).
- **Reading, fixed-field:** the 2025 notes (R12), the 2025 reports and audits (R13) and the model-upgrade trials (R14), each by a smart-tier scout under a closed schema with no quotation and no names. Rows marked *scout reading* below rest on these and are claims until read first-hand.

## Findings

1. **Step-by-step playbooks are the outgrowable kind, with a dated decline.** By the 2025 records they were built to make up for named model failures and worked under the User's enforcement (R12, R13, scout reading). In proportion they fell from as many as six named to one (planning) across Opus 4.7, 4.8, Fable 5 and Opus 5, and its playbook triggers matched almost never and stopped being logged (R4, R9). Rooms reads fell again at the Opus 5.5 switch (R7). The proportion decline runs alongside a steep fall in its activity, and an absent line can also be a change of template, so this finding carries that confound.
2. **Practices that call for judgement rose at the one switch measured.** At rooms' move to Opus 5.5, planning, delegated review, keeping the record and delegation to named agents rose (R7, R8). Two readings complicate it: proportion's named agents per session under Opus 5 fell below their Opus 4.8 level, and rooms' Fable 5.1 sessions made few working-context edits (R7, R8).
3. **Gates keep working and ask less.** Proportion's gates asked the User less from June to July (R9); rooms' gates still block about once or twice a session under the newest models (R8), which includes compliance enforcement such as the spawn-tier gate. *Scout reading:* in rooms' upgrade trials, review and gates still caught defects (R14).
4. **Routing persists, and its settings can move.** In one rooms upgrade trial a mid-tier model handled one stage at about half the cost (R14, scout reading).
5. **The User's authority holds across the eras measured.** At rooms' Opus 5 to 5.5 switch his approvals per prompt held steady (R7); in proportion the approval share ran between about a fifth and three in ten across the months (R3).

## For the sort (plan-bitter-lesson-sort-2026-10-03, slice 0)

The evidence supports sorting by what a part asks of the model (prescribed procedure, or practice that calls for judgement), with review keyed to model releases, which telemetry dates. The playbook decline has already happened in practice, so a without-the-part trial is better aimed at whether the remaining planning playbook adds anything beyond the planning rules. The alternatives and the session's lean are set out in `design-sort-alternatives-2026-10-03.md`.

## Limits

- Model eras coincide with the passage of time, with the User's own practice changing, with the kind of work, and with activity levels; Fable may have been chosen for heavier work, so its figures can mix model with work type.
- Models in the 2025 records are unnamed.
- Per-session comparisons rest on small samples: about a dozen sessions a side at the latest switch, with pause counts of eight against four.
- The 2025 history and the trial results come from scout readings of notes, reports and trial records, and are claims until read first-hand.
- Telemetry carries no file paths, so playbook reads before September 2026 are inferred from the working context and hook triggers.
- No trial removed a part; need is inferred from use, triggers and gate rates.
