# Contributing

This framework grows on evidence from real use. The most valuable contribution is a field report: you ran some part of Memento on real work, and something held, failed or surprised you.

## What is welcome

- **Field reports.** Which step of the graduation ladder you were on, what you fitted, what proved its worth, what you removed and why. Evidence counts for more than opinion; a dated incident counts for more than a preference.
- **Fitting notes.** If you adapted a template to a kind of project the framework has not been used on yet (a research group, a data platform, a solo product), your fitting note is evidence of how well it travels.
- **Corrections with evidence.** This repository ties its claims to supporting records; if you can show a claim is wrong, that correction is the discipline working, and it is wanted.
- **Improvements to templates and documents** that keep them honest, small and easy to fit to a project.

## House rules

- **British English** throughout, and this repository's writing rules apply to the framework's text.
- **Claims trace to evidence.** Say what you observed, where, and how it can be checked. Honest UNVERIFIED labels are welcome, and every confident assertion needs its evidence.
- **No confidential material.** Describe your own projects in general terms if they are private; this repository does the same with its own.
- **Nothing is deleted from the record.** Superseded content gets a banner and is archived; the list of killed mechanisms only grows.

## Mechanics

Issues for questions, reports and corrections; pull requests from a feature branch (`type/short-description`) for changes, with commit messages in `type(scope): summary` form. Discuss significant changes to the governing templates or the story in an issue first, and show the evidence that supports the proposed revision.
