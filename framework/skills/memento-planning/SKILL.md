# Memento planning

This skill brings in the planning playbook at the moment a plan may be due. The playbook is the procedure; this file holds no rule of its own.

1. **Read the project's planning playbook and follow it:** `memento/protocols/playbooks/PLANNING_PLAYBOOK.md` [fit the path to your project].
2. **Apply its trigger (§0).** A plan is required when the undertaking has any of these properties: large; long-horizon; many-stepped or complicated; consequential or hard to reverse. If it has none, say the fixed phrase **"No plan: small, well-specified, reversible"** and proceed.
3. **If a plan is required,** write and save it as the playbook sets out, with every section it requires (§2) and the section for the kind of work (§3), then wait for the User's approval (§5) before any implementation.
